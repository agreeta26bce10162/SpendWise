# Project Statement

## Problem Statement

Managing daily expenses can be difficult, especially for students with a limited monthly budget. If expenses are not recorded properly, it becomes difficult to know where the money is being spent and how much is left.

SpendWise is developed to provide a simple way to record income and expenses, manage a monthly budget, track savings, analyze spending, and generate monthly reports.

## Scope

The scope of SpendWise is to provide basic personal financial management through a simple Python-based system.

The system can:

- Record income and expenses
- Manage monthly budgets
- Display budget alerts
- Analyze spending by category
- Calculate savings
- Generate monthly reports
- Edit, delete, and search transactions

The current version stores data in Python lists while the program is running.

## Target Users

SpendWise is mainly designed for:

- Students
- Individuals managing personal expenses
- Users who want to track their monthly budget and savings

## High-Level Features

- Income management
- Expense management
- Transaction viewing
- Budget management
- Budget alerts
- Spending analysis
- Savings calculation
- Monthly reports
- Transaction editing and deletion
- Transaction search
